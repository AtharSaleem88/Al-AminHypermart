Our Origin

How We
Began

On March 1st, 2023, Ashraf Amin opened the doors of Al-Amin Hypermart with a single, clear conviction that Gujranwala deserved a destination where families could find everything under one roof, without compromise.

What started as a bold vision has become the city's largest retail landmark: 80,000 square feet of space, eight distinct departments, and a team of over 300 dedicated people committed to a standard that puts the customer first, always.

Leadership

"Ashraf Amin"

Founder · Managing Director · CEO
A store is only as good as the trust people place in it. We earn that trust every single day through what we stock, how we price it, and how we treat every person who walks through our doors.

Ashraf Amin leads Al-Amin's operations directly from product sourcing decisions to daily service standards. Under his hands-on oversight since founding day, the business has grown into Gujranwala's largest retail presence, with no signs of slowing.
