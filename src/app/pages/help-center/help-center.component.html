How do I place an order online?

Browse your favorite categories, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. Choose home delivery or store pickup before confirming.

Can I modify or cancel my order?

Yes! You can make changes or cancel your order within 30 minutes of placing it. Please contact our support team immediately.

Is there a minimum order amount?

There is no minimum for store pickup. For delivery, the minimum order is Rs. 3,000.
