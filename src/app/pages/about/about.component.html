OUR ORIGIN · GUJRANWALA · EST. 2023

How we began.One vision. One roof.

Gujranwala's foremost destination for everyday living built on integrity, powered by community.

On March 1st, 2023, Ashraf Amin opened the doors of Al-Amin Hypermart with a single, clear conviction that Gujranwala deserved a destination where families could find everything under one roof, without compromise.

What started as a bold vision has become the city's largest retail landmark: 80,000 square feet of space, eight distinct departments, and a team of over 300 dedicated people committed to a standard that puts the customer first, always.
